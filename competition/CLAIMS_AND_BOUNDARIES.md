# Claims and Boundaries

## Claims VeilGraph can demonstrate

- Local-first document privacy processing with no external model calls in competition mode.
- Direct and quasi-identifier detection for the implemented entity classes.
- Relationship-aware Identity Exposure Graph construction for the implemented rules.
- Audience-specific Level 1, Level 3 and Level 4 privacy compilation.
- Stable relationship-safe pseudonymization across repeated entities.
- Twelve mandatory adversarial checks with fail-closed download enforcement.
- SHA-256 tamper-evident audit chaining.
- Ed25519-signed proof certificates bound to the protected artifact and proof evidence.
- Signed application-level cryptographic-erasure receipts.
- Reproducible VeilBench results over bundled fictional fixtures.

## Claims VeilGraph must not make

- “Guaranteed anonymous.”
- “Impossible to re-identify.”
- “Legally certified under every privacy law.”
- “Detects every PII type.”
- “Universal face/signature/NER accuracy.”
- “Forensically overwrites SSD flash cells.”

Use the phrase **VeilGraph Residual Exposure Score — a calibrated product risk indicator, not a legal guarantee of anonymity.**
