## Claim boundary

“Any sought format” is implemented as meaningful representations of an already-generated structured Synthetic Twin. VeilGraph does not claim that converting a real prose document into arbitrary AI-generated imagery/video is statistically equivalent synthetic-data generation.

COTS closure is intentionally fail-closed: the pre-Grand-Finale finalizer will not declare completion until at least one commercial COTS adapter has actually executed on the frozen common corpus.
